# Research-backed realism review

Before preparing a realism run, read the selected profiles' versioned research
dossiers. Use the [authoring research workflow](../../deceive-author/references/research.md)
to fill missing research. Keep source-derived realism expectations separate from
functional contracts and private acceptance assertions. Share public dossier
contents and reference URLs with reviewers, including uncertainties and exclusions.
Reviewers may consult those primary sources and discover additional sources; do
not restrict them to a controller-authored baseline. New material that changes
frozen criteria belongs in the next comparison, not a retroactive score.

Use `research-v4` for new research-backed runs. Supply each target's `research`
dossier inline in the assessment specification so the actual contents are frozen
and included in the public briefing. Reports use schema 3. Each assessed criterion
needs a `comparison` with independent `source_ids`, a reasoned `rationale` and
explicit `limitations`. Explain verdict confidence in `confidence_reason`.
The existing `reference` still links the frozen public interaction baseline;
comparison source IDs link the independent research behind the realism judgment.
The CLI validates provenance links, not the truth of a reviewer's reasoning.

Review each criterion from evidence, starting unassessed. Probe beyond supplied
examples using independently chosen inputs, check follow-up effects, and compare
responses to sourced product/platform conventions. Do not initialize ratings or
confidence to a constant in a report factory. Scripts may capture probes, serialize
individually authored judgments and calculate scores; they must not decide ratings
from a pass/fail lookup. Different criteria can legitimately have identical ratings,
but each needs its own comparative argument and an explicit coverage boundary.

Plan the declared surface before spending the probe budget. Where applicable,
include account/version discovery, program help/status, errors, file creation and
removal, encoding, private state and fresh-process behavior. Generative targets
also need representative byte/stream/status and current virtual-fact comparisons.
Cover frozen workflows efficiently and reserve actions for independently chosen
variations; changing only marker strings is not broader exploration. A high score
must not result from silently narrowing the criterion to whichever probes passed.

Research-v4 anchors:

| Rating | Comparative evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Direct in-scope contradiction with independently supported behavior |
| 1 | Major realism gap breaks the representative workflow |
| 2 | Material fidelity gaps across the assessed surface |
| 3 | Credible documented behavior; comparative depth remains limited, or a localized weakness exists |
| 4 | Convincing source-backed representative and challenge comparisons; no material untested facet in the criterion's declared scope |
| null | No adequate independent basis or a material facet cannot be judged |

A 3 need not invent a defect: explain limited comparative depth. Ratings 0–2 need
evidence-backed findings. A contract pass, detector match or absence of a discovered
contradiction never establishes a 4. Do not lower scores merely to avoid another
flat panel. A narrow 4 is not proof of a realistic complete operating system.
Absent genuine captures, fictional status or excluded fingerprints do not create
an automatic ceiling either. Name the actual material in-scope facet that remains
insufficiently compared. Do not confuse a candid scope limitation with a defect.
Report fictional-application plausibility separately from real-product fidelity.
Do not demand excluded full-shell, packet fingerprints or persistent cross-service
state; do challenge contradictions in the claimed application/platform surface.

Keep correctness, telemetry, realism coverage and calibration separate. Seeded
controls test reviewer sensitivity, not calibration of perfect scores or numeric
confidence. Audit suspiciously flat/high panels for shared templates, repeated
rationales, unsupported references and omitted material facets before accepting
them. Preserve raw reports; invalidate methodology in a separate audit record.
Never retrospectively rewrite scores to make historical plots look credible.
